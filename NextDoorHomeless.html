Why We Exist

Homelessness is happening next door.

Walk through downtown Battle Creek or downtown Grand Rapids on any given day and within five minutes you will see dozens of our neighbors without a safe place to sleep. They are not statistics. They are people.

Big charities do important work, but it can be hard to see exactly where your money goes. At Next Door Homeless, your donation has one job: to buy something a person needs today.

"Your $5 does not become part of a contribution to some project. It buys 10 bottles of water for the homeless in your neighborhood."

What We Do

Simple programs. Real results.

We focus on what people outside actually need, season by season. No bureaucracy. No waiting lists. Just someone walking up and saying, "Here, this is for you."

April through October

Bottled Water

Staying hydrated in summer heat can be the difference between getting through the day and a medical emergency.

01
Active Program
  • Generic brand bottled water at lowest available unit cost
  • Mobile outreach through downtown neighborhoods
  • Temporary distribution points at rotating public parks
  • $1,000 raised = approximately 2,000 bottles distributed
November through March

Knitwear

A warm beanie or scarf on a cold Michigan night is not a luxury. It is a lifeline for someone sleeping outside.

02
Active Program
  • Beanies and scarves in universal sizes during Years 1 and 2
  • Socks and underwear in M, L, and XL added in Years 2 and 3
  • First distribution event planned before Christmas 2026
  • $1,000 raised = approximately 100 knitwear items distributed
Year 3 and Beyond

Growing Together

As we build trust and funding over the first three years, we will carefully expand into more ways to help.

03
Future Vision
  • Prepared and packaged meal distribution

  • All expansions guided by our public health board member

Our Story

Built by a neighbor, for our neighbors.

Next Door Homeless was founded by Kimi Witherell, a Michigan accountant who looked around her community and decided that the distance between noticing a problem and doing something about it did not have to be very far.

Kimi brings professional financial expertise to the organization, meaning every dollar donated is accounted for with precision and published transparently. What began as a simple idea — give people water and warmth — is becoming a real organization with real reach.

We are currently in our founding phase, recruiting the board members needed to incorporate formally in Michigan. If you believe in this mission and have community experience to offer, we would love to hear from you.

KW
Kimi Witherell
